## §1 — Ashby ultrastability IS antigen's skeleton (REAL-MAP — the biggest finding)

Ashby's **ultrastable system** (*Design for a Brain*, 1952) is a **double feedback loop**:
- **Loop 1 (fast):** parameter feedback keeps the **essential variables** inside **viability bounds**.
- **Loop 2 (slow):** when an essential variable *exits* bounds, a **step-mechanism (the uniselector)
  randomly re-wires structure** until Loop 1 restabilizes.

Antigen, term-by-term:
- **Loop 1** = affinity maturation climbing the Pareto frontier. **Essential variable** = `(recall,
  precision)`. **Viability bound** = the drift threshold.
- **Loop 2** = drift → `ReArm` / `RouteToHuman` → **structural re-declaration** of the antigen.
- **RouteToHuman IS Ashby's uniselector** — the second-loop step-mechanism, with *human judgment
  substituted for Ashby's randomness*.
- **The v0.7 "strange loop"** = *closing Ashby's second loop autonomously.*
- **The conservatism-JOIN** = the governor deciding *when Loop 2 may fire without a human.*

**Consequence (actionable):** *Design for a Brain* is the missing 70-year-old **theory-manual for the v0.7
frontier** — Ashby already worked out the theory of *when a system may safely reorganize itself*, which is
exactly the question the conservatism-JOIN is groping toward. Import its known failure mode: **Loop 2 firing
too eagerly is instability** — the formal ground for keeping `RouteToHuman` the default and the JOIN
conservative.

## §2 — Friston free-energy IS the hinge (REAL-MAP + the sharpest GAP)

The free-energy principle: a system minimizes **free energy = prediction error = surprise**, via two levers —
(1) update the model to fit the world (**perception**), (2) act on the world to fit the model (**active
inference**).

Antigen, term-by-term:
- **Should − Is IS the prediction error.** The GAP field the hinge hands the efferent wing is the free-energy
  gradient (nb 025 §3).
- **Should** = `#[antigen]`/`#[presents]` = the **generative model (prior)**; **Is** = the integrator
  fixpoint = the **recognition density (posterior)**.
- **Afferent wing** (sense + maturation) = **perception**; **efferent wing** (curate / propose / repair) =
  **active inference.** The two-winged body (nb 022) IS Friston's perception/action split, at the deepest
  level.

**THE GAP (REAL-MAP + actionable):** free energy is a **scalar** the organism minimizes. Antigen correctly
refuses a scalar at the *affinity* level (the `(recall, precision)` Pareto vector is honest). But it
**over-generalized that refusal** — there is **no organism-level scalar**, and the *self-organizing*
decisions (which fiber to self-divide, which of the 9 axes to populate next, where to spend attention)
genuinely need one. Anti-scalar is right for the *local* trade-off, wrong for the *global* objective.
**Define an expected-free-energy scalar governing self-organization** — Friston supplies its shape (the
missing objective). Import its failure mode: the **"dark room"** — surprise-minimization collapsing to a
trivial model that predicts nothing hard (for antigen: a repertoire that declares nothing, or only trivial
classes, to keep the gap zero).

## §6 — Dissipative adaptation: RHYME, not import (measure-before-identity, done honestly)

The seductive title ("dissipative adaptation → self-dividing fibrils") was rigorously tested against the
actual physics (Crooks/Jarzynski fluctuation theorems; England's `β⟨Q⟩ ≥ ln(g/δ)` bound) and **failed the
render-math identity test.** Antigen's maturation climb is **deterministic greedy Pareto ascent — no RNG, no
ensemble, no β, no dissipation functional.** The brutal question — *what is physically dissipated?* —
answers **nothing**: the failure-flux drive is a **data stream (information), not free energy**; there is no
reservoir, no work. Two of four non-negotiable physics ingredients are ABSENT; a third (derived selection) is
present-but-**inverted** (antigen *hard-codes* the Pareto objective; physics gets selection *for free*) —
which actually cuts *against* the recognition-not-design claim the resonance was meant to support.

**Verdict: RHYME** — real at the level of English prose, not shared equations. But the rigorous read *earned
its keep* — two genuine leads fell out:
1. **The stochastic-annealing fork** — *if* antigen added an RNG + soft (Metropolis/Boltzmann) acceptance to
   the maturation walk, it becomes a *literal* simulated-annealing / MCMC-on-a-fitness-landscape, and *that*
   field (not England's thermodynamics) has real importable theorems (convergence rates, temperature
   schedules, mixing-time bounds). A design fork for the pathmaker.
2. **The bimodal-cluster self-division criterion** — decidable from the affinity module's own frontier
   geometry: *split a class/fiber when no single Pareto-optimal fingerprint is both tight AND complete, but a
   partition into two lineages closes the recall–precision gap.* The honest, buildable version of
   "self-division" — borrowing only the *word* from the fibrils. (Re-grounds the config-fiber record's
   self-division off the physics.)

Kept as **naming/intuition** (expansionist register, not physics): ceiling-as-depletion-of-improvement,
mutation-budget-decay-as-cooling, compartments-gate-the-drive.
